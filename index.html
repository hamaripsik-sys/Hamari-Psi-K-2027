About the conference

Our community’s meeting, at home in India

  1. 2023NAMMA Psi-kJNCASR & IISc, Bengaluru
  2. 2025AAPALI Psi-kIISER Pune · 19–21 May
  3. 2027HAMARI Psi-kIISER Bhopal · 21–24 July

HAMARI Psi-k 2027 is an international conference and the third edition of the biennial Indian Psi-k series. It brings researchers from India and around the world together at IISER Bhopal for invited talks, contributed talks, poster sessions and focused scientific discussions.

The series began with NAMMA Psi-k, held in Bengaluru in 2023 and hosted by JNCASR and IISc in partnership with the European Psi-k network. It was the first meeting to bring together nearly the entire Indian electronic-structure community. The second edition, AAPALI Psi-k, followed at IISER Pune from 19–21 May 2025. Each meeting is named for the word “ours” in the local language: namma in Kannada, aapali in Marathi and now hamari in Hindi.

The Indian electronic-structure community keeps growing, yet prominent international forums are not equally accessible to everyone. HAMARI Psi-k (हमारी) builds that exchange within India and keeps it open to scholars across the region.

  • Students and early-career researchers meet leading scientists, present their work, get feedback and build collaborations.
  • Exchange across theory, computation and experiment, from catalysis and energy research to quantum materials and molecular science.
  • Broad access, with a particular focus on students, early-career investigators and researchers from developing nations.

Scientific programme

Three themes, three parallel streams

Each day runs three streams of talks in parallel, one per theme, with individual sessions on specific topics within it.

01

Electronic-structure theory, methods & algorithms

  • First-principles software and method development
  • Density-functional and density-matrix functional theories
  • Non-perturbative many-body methods
  • Open and nonequilibrium systems
02

Molecular & materials simulations

  • Phonons and electron–phonon interactions
  • Multiscale and reduced-scaling methods
  • Statistical sampling
  • Molecules, macromolecules and biomolecular systems
03

Catalysis, energy & chemical transformation

  • Catalysis and energy research
  • Quantum materials
  • High-throughput workflows and data analytics
  • AI and machine-learning methods for materials modelling

Special sessions

AI in electronic-structure science

Dedicated sessions on artificial intelligence in electronic-structure theory and materials simulation, from current applications to the questions they raise.

Lecture

“How AI Is Shaping the Future of the Scientific Process”

A philosophical lecture on how AI is changing the way science is done.

Panel discussion

Safe and responsible AI

Policy design for the use of AI in computational electronic-structure research and in scientific publishing.

Young investigators

AI in research & computation in industry

Sessions for young investigators on using AI in research, and on computational research careers in industry.
